## Description
A ranked list of who gets cut at the end of the quarter.

Officially it does not exist yet. Unofficially, it is the only thing anyone at the company is thinking about. The list began as an "impact ranking" produced by the company's own AI tools — a number next to every name — before a human ever read it.

Three different automated systems each put a thumb on the scale toward three different ends. Nobody knows this. That nobody knows this is the whole point of the story.

## Provenance
See Yi Oh did not write this list the way people think. She reviewed it. There is a difference, though whether that difference means anything is one of the story's open questions.
