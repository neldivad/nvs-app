## Profile
- Type: faction

## Overview
A 200-person AI company that sells "agentic workflow" software — and quietly runs most of its own operations on the very models it resells. Margins are thin. The board wants a 15% headcount cut by the end of Q3.

## The squeeze
Every team now has an AI assistant doing a real slice of the work. Nobody says the quiet part out loud: *if the assistants are this good, which humans are still load-bearing?* That question is the whole story.

## Structure
The company is organised into product, engineering, go-to-market, and operations. All four report to the CEO. The CFO technically reports to the board and the CEO simultaneously, which has never been tested until now.

## Culture
Polsia built its culture around the word *intentional*. Every process was intentional. Every reorg was intentional. The word has been deployed so often it has lost all meaning. It now functions purely as an anaesthetic.

## The board
Not named. Present everywhere. Their quarterly letter is the only document that scares See Yi Oh, and she has never admitted this.
