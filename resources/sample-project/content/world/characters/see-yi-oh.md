## Profile
- Role: antagonist
- Status: active
- Age: 41
- Occupation: Chief Executive Officer
- Affiliation: the-company, board-of-directors
- First appearance: ch01-a01-s001

## Appearance
Always put together. The kind of composed that reads as either confidence or numbness depending on context.

## Personality
Skilled operator. Not cruel but not kind either — functional. Genuinely believes in what she's doing up to the moment she doesn't.

## Voice
- Style: Warm, deliberate, always asking for confirmation she doesn't need. Manages up while appearing to manage down.
- Quirks: uses meeting-speak as camouflage; makes bad news sound like housekeeping
- Never says: layoffs, restructure, difficult
- Sample lines:
  - "Can people see the slide? Just drop a yes in the chat."
  - "Thanks, Hugh. Let's give it a sec for folks to trickle in."

## Arc
### Goal
- Execute the restructure without losing control of the narrative

### Wound
- Was herself restructured out of a previous role; swore it would never happen to her again

### Obstacle
- The company genuinely cannot be saved the way she's trying to save it

### Lie
- If the communication is perfect, the outcome will be acceptable

### Truth
- There is no communication strategy for a bad decision

## Notes
Yi Oh is not a villain. She is a person who has internalised the logic of institutions so completely she can no longer tell the difference between the institution's needs and her own.
