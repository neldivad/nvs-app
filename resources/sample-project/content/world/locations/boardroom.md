## Overview
A glass-walled conference room on the top floor. Big enough to hold sixty people standing, forty sitting. Usually holds twelve.

## Description
Long table. Too many chairs. A projector that works fine until it doesn't. The kind of room that makes everything said inside it feel official, even when it isn't.

Cold in the mornings. The HVAC is on a timer nobody has ever figured out.
