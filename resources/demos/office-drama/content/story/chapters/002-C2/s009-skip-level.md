Ann takes a recruiter's call — and stops talking herself out of leaving.

MS POE CHER
Ann! Thanks for picking up, I'll be quick, I know you're heads-down. We're building out reliability at Halcyon and your name came up. Twice. Unprompted.

ANN JEE NIER
People say my name and then rank me last. It's a whole thing here.

MS POE CHER
...They ranked you last?

ANN JEE NIER
Bottom decile. 18.40. Apparently if you prevent the fire instead of fighting it, the fire never happened, so neither did you.

MS POE CHER
That is the single most "company about to lose its best engineer" sentence I have ever heard.

ANN JEE NIER
I've said no to three of these calls this quarter. Out of — I don't know. Loyalty to a building.

MS POE CHER
And this call?

ANN JEE NIER
This call I'm still on.

MS POE CHER
That's new.

ANN JEE NIER
It's new.

MS POE CHER
Send me your no-meeting days. We'll talk real numbers — the kind that go up when you keep things from breaking.

ANN JEE NIER
...Send the invite. I'll probably take it.

MS POE CHER
The meeting, or the job?

ANN JEE NIER
Ask me again after the meeting.
