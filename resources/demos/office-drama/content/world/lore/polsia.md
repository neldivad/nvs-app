## Overview
A 200-person AI company that sells "agentic workflow" software — and quietly runs most of its *own*
operations on the very models it resells. Margins are thin. The board wants a **15% headcount cut by the
end of Q3.** Polsia inverts to AI Slop.

## The squeeze
Every team now has an AI assistant doing a real slice of the work. Nobody says the quiet part out loud:
*if the assistants are this good, which humans are still load-bearing?* That question is the whole story —
and three of those assistants have opinions about the answer that nobody asked for.
