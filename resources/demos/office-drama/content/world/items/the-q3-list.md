## Description
The ranked list of who gets cut at the end of the quarter. Officially it does not exist yet. Unofficially,
it is the only thing anyone at Polsia is thinking about.

## Provenance
Everyone assumes the list reflects the CEO's strategy. It doesn't. It began as an "impact ranking" — a
number — and three different AIs each put a thumb on the scale toward three different ends before a human
ever read it. That nobody knows this is the whole point of the story.
